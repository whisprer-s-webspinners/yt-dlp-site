Architecture note

    
This is a VPS agent secured by Cloudflare Tunnel + Zero Trust Access. Downloads run on your server. The UI is public but login-protected by Cloudflare. In other words not only have we gone to some efforts to protect our stuff from being banned/blocked/pulled down, we've also gone to efforts to stop your login being caused any problems too!
